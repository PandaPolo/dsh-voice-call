dsh-voice — promo narration (~70 seconds, 7 slides)
====================================================
Read this aloud while screen-recording. Each block starts on its slide; hold
each slide ~9-10 seconds. Advance with the space bar or right arrow. Total
run time ≈ 70 seconds.

[Slide 1 — hero]
Meet dsh-voice: voice notes in, spoken answers out. Dictate what you need,
and the agent reads its replies aloud. A hands-free terminal for DSH.

[Slide 2 — what it is]
Two tools. transcribe turns spoken audio into a real user message — the
agent answers it like anything you typed. speak reads text aloud on a
background job, without ever blocking the turn.

[Slide 3 — the pain]
Two everyday moments a terminal handles badly. You're away from the
keyboard and want to leave an instruction. Or you're mid-task and don't
want to read a wall of output.

[Slide 4 — the fix]
So dsh-voice fixes both. Dictate a note — it becomes a user turn, with an
audio card you can replay. Ask for the reply aloud — it is synthesized and
spoken while you keep working.

[Slide 5 — local-first]
Everything stays local. Audio is plain files under tilde-slash-dsh-slash
voice — inspect them, delete them. The session log holds only refs and
transcripts. Nothing auto-runs, and audio only leaves your machine if you
configure a cloud backend.

[Slide 6 — details]
Narration doubles as walk-away status on long builds. Slash-voice on flips
spoken replies on live. Offline backends first: whisper, macOS speech, say,
piper.

[Slide 7 — install]
One command to install: dsh plugin, profile web, add, at-dsh-voice-slash-
bundle. Voice notes in. Spoken answers out.
